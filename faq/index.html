Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and learning support.

Who can use MentorSpark Solutions?

Students, job seekers and working IT professionals can contact us about our services.

Are sessions online?

Our services are designed to be delivered online. Session details are confirmed before booking.

Which technologies do you cover?

Course and support areas include Java, Python, web development, React, SQL, cloud, DevOps, testing and data-related topics. Availability is confirmed during enquiry.

How much do services cost?

Pricing depends on the service and scope. Interview preparation has a previously discussed rate of US$150 per hour; confirm the current rate with our team.

Can I choose a schedule?

Share your preferred schedule. The team will confirm availability before a session is booked.

Do you guarantee a job or interview result?

No. We provide preparation, learning and guidance. Hiring decisions and outcomes depend on many factors outside our control.

How do I get started?

Use the contact form and tell us which service you are interested in.

Can I ask about a course not listed?

Yes. Contact us with the technology or topic you want to learn.

Ready to take your next step?

Tell us what you want to learn or prepare for. We will discuss a suitable next step.
